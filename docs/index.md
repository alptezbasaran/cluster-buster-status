## Phase 1 — complete

Every config in the Phase-1 grid, coloured by where it is in the pipeline. All 126 sets
were uploaded, classified and retired; volunteers finished the last subject on 21 October 2026.
Two panels for the `ratio_limit` split; hover any cell for its set and subject count.

## Phase 2 — live

Phase 2 varies all six clustering knobs together, including the three Phase 1 held fixed: 64 configs
in four waves of 16, on the same 500 galaxies and the same manually cleaned tracings
(see [The Investigation](/investigation#what-we-sweep-—-phase-2)). Wave 1 (sets 127–142, 7,846
subjects) went live on 22 October 2026; follow it on the [Sets](/sets) page.

## Why this exists

Spiral arms wind at a characteristic **pitch angle** that tracks the mass of a galaxy's
central black hole — but measuring it depends on first **clustering volunteer arm tracings**
correctly, and that clustering has knobs. Cluster Buster sweeps those knobs (126 configs in
Phase 1, 64 more in Phase 2) and lets Zooniverse volunteers judge which settings actually
produce good arms.
